For LevrAge Innovation Studios, the best fit is a calm, dark, technical aesthetic with one restrained “wow” moment that signals you’re serious but not flashy.

Color scheme
Base: Very dark slate / charcoal background (e.g. near‑black with a hint of blue).
​

Primary accent: Electric teal or cyan for CTAs and key highlights (ties to “network intelligence” and infra).

Secondary accent: Soft amber or desaturated red for warning/important states (not as a main brand color).

Neutrals:

Light gray for body text

Off‑white for headings

Muted borders/dividers in mid‑gray

This gives a studio / infra feel, similar to modern SaaS palettes but a touch more serious and minimal.
​

Tech stack
Framework: Next.js (app router) for fast, SEO‑friendly, React‑based pages.
​

Styling: Tailwind CSS + a light component layer (e.g. shadcn/ui or your own minimal system) for rapid iteration.
​
​

Deployment: Vercel for frictionless deploys and previews.

Forms: Simple custom form posting to a serverless function or a lightweight form backend (e.g. formspree / basin) to avoid over‑engineering.

Analytics: Privacy‑friendly basic analytics (Plausible / Umami) to avoid heavy dashboards.

Everything should be easily clonable / modifiable, matching your “I can spin this up quickly for you too” story.

Overall feel
Mood: Calm, precise, “ops‑minded builder,” not hype or maximalist.

Layout:

Single column with generous spacing and tight grids for the flagships and client MVPs.

Typography:

One strong sans font for headings (slightly condensed).

One very readable sans/neo‑grotesk for body (no quirky type).

Micro‑details:

Subtle card outlines and soft shadows, not glassmorphism.
