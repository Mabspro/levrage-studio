LevrAge Innovation Studios — Pricing Overview

This document outlines how LevrAge Innovation Studios prices early-stage product builds.
Pricing is presented in ranges, not fixed menus, because first versions vary in complexity, risk, and integration depth.

How We Price

We price based on outcomes and systems, not pages or hours.

Every engagement accounts for:

architecture decisions

backend and data models

authentication and access control

hosting and deployment

iteration and stabilization

a clear path to ownership

AI is used internally to accelerate delivery, but pricing reflects judgment, accountability, and real-world execution, not keystrokes.

Engagement Types
POC Sprint

Typical range: $2,500 – $6,500

Best for validating a core idea with something people can actually use.

Includes

One primary user flow end-to-end

Clickable, working prototype (not slides)

Basic backend and data structure

Optional lightweight auth (if needed)

Deployed and shareable

Not intended for

Full feature sets

Complex role systems

Production-scale reliability

Typical timeline

1–2 weeks

MVP Launch

Typical range: $8,500 – $20,000

Best for products that need to support real users, not just demos.

Includes

Authentication and user roles

Database schema and core data model

One or two critical workflows fully implemented

Hosting, environments, and deployment setup

One primary third-party integration (if needed)

Basic monitoring and error awareness

Ideal when

Users will sign in

Data matters

You need to learn from real usage

Typical timeline

3–6 weeks

MVP+ / Integration-Heavy or Regulated Builds

Typical range: $20,000 – $45,000+

Accepted selectively.

Best for domains with higher complexity or risk, such as:

healthcare

finance

regulated workflows

advanced integrations (video, payments, EHRs, CRMs, etc.)

Includes

Deeper access control and role separation

More rigorous data handling patterns

Integration planning with future compliance in mind

Slower, more deliberate iteration

These builds require additional discovery and scoping.

Launchpad Ops (Optional Monthly Support)

For teams that want short-term help running and stabilizing their first version.

Ops Lite

$750 – $1,500 / month

Hosting oversight

Minor fixes and adjustments

Light content or configuration updates

Best-effort response (not 24/7 support)

Ops Plus

$2,000 – $4,000 / month

Everything in Ops Lite

Dedicated iteration bandwidth (limited hours/month)

Priority response

Light roadmap guidance

Launchpad Ops is intentionally temporary.
The goal is graduation, not dependency.

What Affects Pricing

Pricing within a range depends on:

number of user roles

depth of data modeling

integration surface area

domain sensitivity (health, finance, etc.)

timeline urgency

You will always know where and why your project falls within a range before work begins.

What’s Intentionally Out of Scope

LevrAge Innovation Studios is optimized for first versions.

The studio does not position itself as:

a long-term dev shop

a scale engineering team

a large multi-team delivery partner

When a product outgrows the studio, we help transition it cleanly into accounts and teams you own.
